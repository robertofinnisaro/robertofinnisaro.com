Glaurung is a heavily modified Creality Ender 3 V2, named, like the lab's other
printers, after a dragon. The original bed-slinger frame has been
rebuilt as a CoreXY machine running Klipper, with a Stealthburner printhead, a
Phaetus Dragon HF hotend and a bed probe. Multi-material printing is handled by a
four-lane Box Turtle.

The build volume is taken from the OrcaSlicer machine preset.
